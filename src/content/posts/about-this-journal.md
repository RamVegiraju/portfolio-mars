This journal brings the different parts of this site together: professional work in AI and machine learning, an interest in boxing, videos, and more personal writing.

Most of the writing here will be personal. There is also room for an occasional technical post, a useful lesson, or a short update that does not need to become a long essay.

## What belongs here

- **Life:** personal topics and reflections.
- **Boxing:** writing about an interest beyond work.
- **AI/ML:** occasional technical notes and ideas.
- **Updates:** shorter posts about what is new.

The [video library](/videos/) is home to videos and learning paths. For the professional background, visit [Professional](/professional/) or [read the resume](/resume.pdf). [Boxing](/boxing/) has its own space for combat sports and related writing.

This is a short introduction to the site. More writing will follow.
